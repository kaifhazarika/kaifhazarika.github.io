Profile

A physicist working the boundary between upper-atmosphere research and applied systems.

My research foundation is in upper-atmosphere and space physics — built through undergraduate and postgraduate work on thermospheric wind response to geomagnetic storms, and equatorial E×B drift dynamics using C/NOFS satellite data.

That foundation extends outward: a state-government Climate Resilient Village Fellowship and a satellite-data case study on climate change's effect on rural livelihoods took me into applied climate research, while an ongoing Master's in Library & Information Science and two filed patent applications took me into information systems and applied engineering.

I move comfortably between physical modeling, scientific computing, and applied data analysis — working in Python, Fortran, C++, and Java, with domain tools spanning MRST-co2lab, GNOME, Grapher, GPS-X, and Design-Expert.
